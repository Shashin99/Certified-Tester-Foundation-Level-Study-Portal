    <div class="quiz-card">
      <div class="quiz-q">Question 1 [K3 - FL-5.1.4]: Using the Three-Point Estimation technique, experts provide the following test execution estimates: Most Optimistic (a) = 10 hours, Most Likely (m) = 16 hours, and Most Pessimistic (b) = 34 hours. What are the final weighted Estimate (E) and Standard Deviation (SD)?</div>
      <div class="quiz-options">
        <div class="quiz-opt">A) E = 18 hours, SD = 4 hours</div>
        <div class="quiz-opt">B) E = 20 hours, SD = 4 hours</div>
        <div class="quiz-opt">C) E = 18 hours, SD = 6 hours</div>
        <div class="quiz-opt">D) E = 16 hours, SD = 2 hours</div>
      </div>
      <div class="quiz-answer">
        <strong>Correct Answer: A</strong><br>
        <strong>Rationale:</strong> Using PERT formulas:
        <br>&bull; <b>E</b> = (a + 4m + b) / 6 = (10 + 4(16) + 34) / 6 = (10 + 64 + 34) / 6 = 108 / 6 = <b>18 hours</b>
        <br>&bull; <b>SD</b> = (b &minus; a) / 6 = (34 &minus; 10) / 6 = 24 / 6 = <b>4 hours</b>
        <br>Thus, the final estimate is <strong>18 &plusmn; 4 hours</strong> (Section 5.1.4).
      </div>
    </div>
