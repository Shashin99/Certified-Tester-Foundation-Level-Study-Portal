Master Library Architecture, Exam Blueprint & Study Strategy

Authoritative syllabus breakdown, chapter weights, study roadmap, and catalog for the ISTQB® CTFL v4.0.1 examination.

Primary Exam Standard: ISTQB® CTFL v4.0.1

The official examination contains exactly 40 multiple-choice questions to be answered in 60 minutes (90 seconds per question). The passing threshold is 65% (26 out of 40 correct). There is no penalty for incorrect answers.

1. Official Chapter Question Allocation & Cognitive K-Levels

Chapter Title Question Allocation Cognitive Levels Recommended Study Focus
Chapter 01 Fundamentals of Testing 8 Questions (20%) K1 (Remember), K2 (Understand) 7 Testing Principles, Error vs Defect vs Failure, Whole Team Approach, Testware Traceability
Chapter 02 Testing Throughout the SDLC 6 Questions (15%) K1 (Remember), K2 (Understand) V-Model vs Agile, Shift-Left, TDD/ATDD/BDD, 5 Test Levels, 4 Test Types, Maintenance Testing
Chapter 03 Static Testing 4 Questions (10%) K1 (Remember), K2 (Understand) Static vs Dynamic, Formal Review Process (ISO/IEC 20246), 6 Review Roles, 4 Review Types
Chapter 04 Test Analysis & Design 11 Questions (27.5%) K1, K2, K3 (Apply) Equivalence Partitioning, BVA (2-point vs 3-point), Decision Tables, State Transitions, Statement & Branch Coverage
Chapter 05 Managing the Test Activities 9 Questions (22.5%) K1, K2, K3 (Apply) Risk Management (Likelihood x Impact), Test Pyramid, Testing Quadrants, Metrics, Defect Reports
Chapter 06 Test Tools 2 Questions (5%) K1 (Remember), K2 (Understand) Tool Classification, Spreadsheet Principle, Automation Benefits & Risks, Pilot Projects

High-Yield Notice: Chapters 4 and 5 together represent 50% of the entire exam (20 out of 40 questions). Master these two chapters thoroughly to ensure passing.

2. Recommended 4-Stage Study Roadmap

Stage 01
Foundations & Lifecycle
Build absolute clarity on foundational definitions, terminology, and development integration.
  • Read Chapter 01 & Chapter 02 Study Guides
  • Memorize the 7 Principles & Error/Defect/Failure
  • Review Test Levels, Types & Shift-Left approach
Stage 02
Static & Dynamic Design (K3)
Master review procedures and all high-yield calculation techniques.
  • Read Chapter 03 & Chapter 04 Study Guides
  • Practice 2-point vs 3-point Boundary Value Analysis
  • Build Decision Tables & State Transition tables
  • Calculate Statement Coverage & Branch Coverage
Stage 03
Management, Risk & Tools
Learn test leadership, metrics, risk analysis, and automation factors.
  • Read Chapter 05 & Chapter 06 Study Guides
  • Understand Risk Matrix (Likelihood x Impact)
  • Study Test Pyramid & Agile Testing Quadrants
  • Memorize Automation Benefits & Risks
Stage 04
Mock Exams & Calibration
Simulate realistic testing conditions with timed mock tests.
  • Practice all 71 questions in the Question Bank
  • Complete multiple runs of the 40Q Exam Simulator
  • Target >= 85% score (34/40) before taking the real exam
