3. RECOMMENDED 4-STAGE STUDY ROADMAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1: FOUNDATIONS & LIFECYCLES (Days 1 - 3)
  * Focus: Chapters 1 and 2 (~14 Questions)
  * Key Topics to Master:
    - The 7 Testing Principles (Principles 1 through 7, especially Fallacy & Wear Out).
    - Error (human mistake) -> Defect (bug in code/doc) -> Failure (incorrect behavior).
    - Testing vs. Debugging difference.
    - V-Model vs Agile iterative lifecycles, Shift-Left mindset, and Test-First (TDD/ATDD/BDD).
    - 4 Test Levels (Component, Integration, System, Acceptance) & 4 Test Types.
  * Milestone: Score >= 80% on Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 end-of-chapter quizzes.

STAGE 2: STATIC TESTING & APPLIED TEST TECHNIQUES (Days 4 - 7) [HIGHEST PRIORITY]
  * Focus: Chapters 3 and 4 (~15 Questions)
  * Key Topics to Master:
    - Static vs Dynamic testing value & defects found earlier by static testing.
    - Review process (Planning, Initiate, Individual review, Issue communication, Fixing).
    - Review roles: Author, Management, Facilitator (Moderator), Review Leader, Reviewer, Scribe.
    - Review types: Informal review, Walkthrough, Technical review, Inspection.
    - Black-Box K3 Techniques:
      * Equivalence Partitioning (Valid vs Invalid partitions, min test cases for 100% coverage).
      * Boundary Value Analysis (2-point: boundary & invalid neighbor; 3-point: boundary & both neighbors).
      * Decision Table Testing (Conditions, Actions, Rules calculation).
      * State Transition Testing (States, transitions, invalid transitions, state tables).
    - White-Box K3 Techniques: Statement coverage vs Branch coverage (Branch coverage subsumes Statement).
    - Experience-based: Error Guessing, Exploratory Testing, Checklist-based testing.
  * Milestone: Work through all practical calculation exercises in Chapter 4 study guide.

STAGE 3: TEST MANAGEMENT, RISK & TOOLS (Days 8 - 10)
  * Focus: Chapters 5 and 6 (~11 Questions)
  * Key Topics to Master:
    - Test Planning, Test Strategy, and Test Approach.
    - Estimation techniques: Metrics-based vs Expert-based (Wideband Delphi, Planning Poker).
    - Test Case Prioritization (Risk-based, dependency-based, value-based).
    - Test Pyramid (Unit -> Service -> UI) & Agile Testing Quadrants (Q1-Q4).
    - Product Risk vs Project Risk analysis (Likelihood x Impact).
    - Entry Criteria vs Exit Criteria (Definition of Ready vs Definition of Done).
    - Defect reports: mandatory fields, objective language, reproduce steps.
    - Test Tools classification, Spreadsheet principle, 6 automation benefits & 6 automation risks.
  * Milestone: Score >= 85% on Chapter 5 and Chapter 6 quizzes.

STAGE 4: QUESTION BANK MASTERY & TIMED SIMULATOR PRACTICE (Days 11 - 14)
  * Master all 71 questions in question_bank.txt.
  * Complete at least three 60-minute timed runs on istqb_mock_exam_simulator.html.
  * Target score: >= 32 / 40 (80%+) consistently before booking official exam.

5. EXAM-DAY STRATEGY & TEST-TAKING TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pace Yourself: 60 minutes for 40 questions = 90 seconds per question.
2. First Pass Strategy: Answer all straightforward K1 and K2 recall questions first.
   Flag complex K3 calculation questions (BVA, Decision Tables) for the second pass.
3. Watch the Exact Wording:
   - Pay extreme attention to negative keywords: NOT, EXCEPT, DISADVANTAGE, LEAST likely.
   - "Which TWO..." means select exactly two options.
4. Eliminate Distractors:
   - Eliminate absolute words ("always", "never", "guarantees zero defects").
   - Eliminate choices confusing testing with debugging (debugging removes defects; testing finds failures).
5. Never Leave Any Question Blank: No negative marking exists. Even a guess gives a 25% chance.
